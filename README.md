# Moamen Abdelkawy: personal website

Source for my professional profile at [moamen-abdelkawy.github.io](https://moamen-abdelkawy.github.io).

I'm a Senior Economist at National Navigation Company in Cairo. I use econometrics and machine learning to measure how economic shocks move shipping, commodity, and financial markets, and to forecast where they go next. I hold an MSc in Financial Engineering from WorldQuant University and have 19 years of experience in economic research, quantitative modeling, and university teaching.

## Updating the page

All content is plain HTML and CSS, so no build step is needed.

- **New role:** copy an `<li class="role">` block in the Experience section of `index.html`. Move the `role--current` class to the newest role so its marker stays highlighted.
- **New article or project:** copy an existing `<li>` in the Writing section or an `<article class="project">` block in Projects.
- **New résumé:** upload the new PDF with the same filename (`Moamen_Abdelkawy_Resume.pdf`).

The fan chart at the top of `index.html` is a decorative illustration of a forecast; empirical figures appear in `panamax-forecasting.html`.
